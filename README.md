Toqio is an embedded finance platform that lets corporates and financial institutions design, launch, and scale financial products — accounts, payments, cards, lending, and expense management — for their SME customers without building infrastructure. The platform pairs a no-code/low-code Design Studio with a REST API (OAuth 2.0 client credentials) covering accounts, transactions, beneficiaries, cards, fees, compliance/KYB, revenue collection, and notifications, plus an Integration Hub through which banks and BaaS providers plug their services into the Toqio marketplace.

**Areas:** Embedded Finance · Fintech · Banking as a Service · Payments · Cards · Corporate Banking · Lending · Compliance · Small Business

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Toqio: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
